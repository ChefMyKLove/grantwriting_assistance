How the funding works

Field 1

You self-identify

Deaf, hard of hearing, disability, mental illness, or facing language/geographic/cultural barriers as a First Nations, Inuit, or Métis artist. One form, on your own Canada Council portal account. No documentation required.

Field 2

You name me as your support person

You request Application Assistance and list me as the provider. I'm approved per-case, not pre-registered — this happens for each application.

Field 3

I do the work, on your portal

You grant me third-party access to your specific application. I write, edit, and organize inside your account — nothing happens without your login controlling it.

Field 4

Canada Council pays — not you

Once your application is submitted, I invoice Canada Council directly. Covered hours cost you nothing.

You don't need a pension, a registered diagnosis, or anyone's approval to qualify. The Self-ID form on the Canada Council portal is the only gate — and it's yours to complete, in your own words, at your own pace.
